# Content sourcing and fact decisions

All organizational descriptions and dates were checked against the official INTERalliance pages in November 2026. External websites, future events, team membership, and applications can change; review before any official presentation.

## Official pages

- [INTERalliance Impact Report](https://interalliance.org/about-us/impact-report/) — milestones (2007 founding, 2008 ILC, 2009 first interns, 2010 first TechOlympics, 2015 Women in IT collaboration, 2017 IT Intern Summit collaboration), INTER framework, and descriptions of Chapters, TechOlympics, JumpStart, internships, WiIT, and ILC.
- [INTERalliance Home](https://interalliance.org/) — organization's current mission and student-led vision.
- [INTERalliance Leadership Council](https://interalliance.org/programs/ilc/) — ILC duties and six teams: Systems, Data, Programs, Marketing, Chapters, Finance.
- [INTERalliance Chapters](https://interalliance.org/chapters/) — school chapter activities and opportunities.
- [INTERalliance Programs](https://interalliance.org/programs/) — high-level program navigation.

## Editorial decisions

- The primary public **Impact Report currently identifies 2007** as the founding year. Some older materials have differed; the game attributes the date to the Impact Report, so review it with INTERalliance before official reuse.
- The game does not show live metrics, team member counts, application windows, or internship eligibility, because they can change.
- "BUILD / LEAD / LAUNCH" is a **game-designed orientation framework**, not an official INTERalliance classification.
- Completing the game does not confer membership, employment, or admission into ILC.
- The technology is an independent educational demo and does not imply partnership or endorsement.

## Level 2 learning mechanics

- The history timeline uses 2007 founding → 2008 ILC → 2010 TechOlympics. The first INTERnships (2009), Women in IT (2015), and Intern Summit (2017) are also taught in the facts carousel.
- The operations desk routes a crashed website to Systems, duplicate registrations to Data, and a sponsor budget to Finance. These are **illustrative scenarios**, not claims that those incidents occurred.
