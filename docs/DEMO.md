# ILC demo: 2-minute walkthrough

## Before the meeting

1. Use the hosted HTTPS version on a laptop; test the link and each of the five exhibits.
2. If a VR headset is available, test entering VR, controller selection, and the board fact carousel **on that exact device**.
3. Have a laptop fallback ready. Headset instability should not derail the pitch.
4. Click **Reset** to start from zero.
5. Confirm with your ILC contact that content and event names are accurate before treating it as official.

## Pitch script (approximately 2 minutes)

**Opening (15 seconds)**

"I wanted to make it easier for students to understand what INTERalliance actually is. Instead of introducing it with another slide deck, I built a VR orientation prototype."

**Show the history (25 seconds)**

"This first room tells the actual history—ILC, the first interns, TechOlympics, and later programs. Players actually rebuild the sequence by selecting three floating historical time capsules from earliest to latest."

**Show opportunities and leadership (40 seconds)**

"The following exhibits explain why the organization exists, what students can do, and how ILC's six teams operate. At the ILC operations desk, players route a website emergency, a data-cleanup request, and a budgeting task to the correct teams. These are simulated, not historical incidents."

**Show personalization (20 seconds)**

"At the end, every new student gets a starting path based on their interests: building, leadership, or career development."

**Close (20 seconds)**

"This is an early prototype, not an official INTERalliance product. I'd love to contribute to refining it into something chapters could reuse during recruitment, and I'd welcome technical or content feedback."

## Answering honest objections

- **"Is this finished?"** — It's an interactive prototype with complete content, tests, and a desktop fallback; physical VR hardware testing still needs to happen.
- **"Is this official?"** — No. The material is sourced from INTERalliance's website, and the team would need to review it before official use.
- **"What would you do on ILC?"** — Improve usability, collaborate with Programs and Chapters on content, and support deployment and iteration with Systems.
- **"Why VR?"** — It makes the introduction experiential and memorable, while the browser version keeps it accessible to students without headsets.

## Recommended live demo path (under two minutes)

1. **Start at History**: activate 2007, then 2008, then 2010. This demonstrates a real interactive puzzle.
2. **Press 4**: jump to ILC HQ and route the three simulated tickets: Systems, Data, Finance.
3. **Press 5**: choose LEAD. The personalized result and official ILC link appear when all checkpoints are cleared; for a short demo, show the result in a separate pre-cleared browser profile, or describe it without claiming it is unlocked.
4. If the 3D engine or headset fails, append `?desktop=1` to the URL and continue in the branded desktop interface.

Avoid spending half the meeting clicking through ten questions. Demonstrate two mechanics, explain the complete journey, then discuss the team's needs.
